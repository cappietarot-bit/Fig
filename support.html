Why does everyone get the same cards?

Everyone sees the same three cards on the same day, so you can talk about them with the people you share Fig with. New cards arrive every morning.

How do I read the whole chapter?

Tap a card until you reach the verse it rests on, then tap the verse or Read the chapter. The card's verse is highlighted, and Previous and Next move between chapters.

My streak started over

Your streak counts the days in a row you open Fig. Missing a day starts it again from one.

How do I change the look or the music?

Tap the swatch at the top right to choose Bloom, Cedar, Iron or Linen. Tap the music note to turn the piano on or off.
