Fig

Privacy policy, last updated October 19, 2026

The short version. Fig does not collect any personal information. There is no account, no sign in, no ads and no analytics.

What stays on your device

Your streak, the cards you save, the look you choose and whether music is on are kept only on your device. They are never sent to us. Deleting the app deletes them.

What the app loads

The cards, the King James Bible and the music are built into the app. The app's fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives the basic information any web request carries, such as your IP address. We never receive or store it. Google explains how it handles that in its privacy policy.

Children

Fig is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
